Suggested description:
This archive contains the MATLAB code, shared numerical data, and figure outputs used to reproduce Figs. 2–9 of the manuscript “Position-Controlled Nonlinear Energy-Harvesting AF Relaying for 6G Networks: A Unified Framework for Movable and Pinching Antennas.” The package includes the exact deterministic hop-specific movable-antenna spatial realization, pinching-antenna numerical model, nonlinear energy-harvesting AF-relay calculations, Monte Carlo validation, optimization routines, and figure-generation scripts. Figure 1 is included as the system-model schematic and is not MATLAB-generated.
